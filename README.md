## Approach

The text slows the reader down without ever telling them to. It opens on one small, concrete scene — the tide over flat sand — and lets the image widen in three steps: the pools left behind, the sea's old rhythm far out, and finally the night sky holding more stars than any eye could count, so the calm keeps growing after the last line. The sentences are long and soft-sounding, built from plain words (sand, water, light, shore, stars) with repeated s-sounds and unhurried commas, so the reading pace settles on its own. Nothing in it instructs or prescribes; the stillness comes from what the scene shows.
